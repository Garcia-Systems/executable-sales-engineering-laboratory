# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for differing viewpoints, and
gracefully accepting responsibility. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory
language, personal or political attacks, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing another
person's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers will clarify and enforce these standards and may remove, edit, or reject
contributions that violate them. Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository's
security contact or maintainers. Reports will be reviewed promptly, fairly, and confidentially.
